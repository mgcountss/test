How I work

A simple, repeatable way of working

Borrowed from operations: understand the system before you change it, then measure whether the change worked.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Sit with the people doing the work. Map the real process and where it breaks — not the one on the org chart.

  2. 02

    Model

    Turn that into a clear data model and a small, honest spec everyone can agree on before a line of code.

  3. 03

    Build

    Ship the smallest useful version fast — a working tool beats a perfect plan. Automate the tedious parts.

  4. 04

    Measure

    Instrument it, watch how it performs at scale, and iterate. Leave the process simpler and better-documented than I found it.
